# Snooker Clip

**An Android app that automatically captures, analyzes, and lets you share your best snooker shots in real time.**

### Main Features

* 🎥 **Automatic Shot Capture** — Detects every shot and saves it as a separate video clip.
* 🎯 **Cue Ball Tracking** — Tracks the cue ball and shows its final position and trajectory.
* 📊 **Built-in Scoreboard** — Record scores and breaks while playing.
* ⭐ **Highlight Sharing** — Select your best shots and create a highlight video to share.
* 🎬 **Video File Processing** — Create snooker highlights from previously recorded videos.
* 📡 **IP Camera Support** — Connect to an IP camera over your local network using RTSP.
* ⚡ **Fast Local Processing** — Video processing and AI analysis run directly on the device.
* 🔒 **100% Local & Private** — Your videos never leave your device; no cloud storage or Internet connection is required.
* 💻 **Works on Entry-Level Devices** — Designed to run smoothly on affordable Android devices.
* 🎯 **No Manual Calibration** — Automatically adapts to different tables and lighting conditions.
